Privacy Policy

Wiz RingTask by Wiz Labs · Effective 21 November 2026

Summary

Wiz RingTask has no accounts and sends none of your reminders, voice notes or contacts anywhere. Your reminders, voice recordings and call history stay on your phone. The app rings like an incoming call at the time you choose; it does not place real phone calls.

What the app does

You write a reminder (who "calls", when, how often, and what to say). At that time the app rings with a call-style screen. When you answer, it reads the reminder aloud with your phone's text-to-speech voice, or plays a voice note you recorded, and shows the text on screen. You can snooze, finish, or ignore it; ignored calls are shown as missed and may ring again a few times.

Permissions

  • Exact alarms (USE_EXACT_ALARM): to ring at the exact minute you chose. This is the core function of the app.
  • Full-screen notifications (USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT): to show the call screen when the phone is locked. Without it, a banner is shown instead.
  • Notifications: to ring and to tell you about missed reminder calls. Lock-screen notifications show only "Incoming reminder call", not your reminder text.
  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO), optional: only while you press Record to make a voice note for a reminder. The recording is stored on your phone, is never uploaded, and nothing is recorded in the background.
  • Run after restart (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): to restore your reminders after the phone restarts, or when the time or time zone changes.
  • Vibrate and wake lock: to vibrate and light the screen when a call comes in.
  • Internet access: used only by Google Play for app updates and review prompts. RingTask itself sends nothing about your reminders.

The app does not request contacts, phone, call-log or location permissions. When you tap the contact button, Android shows its own contact picker and shares only the one name you pick.

What we collect

  • Reminders, voice notes, call history and settings: stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage. We never receive them.
  • Backups: "Save a backup" writes one file (reminders and voice notes) to a place you choose. Restoring reads a file you choose. Android may also back up your settings (not your reminders or voice notes).
  • Analytics, accounts, advertising IDs: none.

Third-party components

The app includes Google Play services libraries for in-app updates and review prompts, and a Google ad library that is not active in this version. These may process technical data such as device and diagnostic information under Google's privacy policy. If ads are added in a future version, this policy and the in-app consent form will be updated first. Text-to-speech uses the voice engine already installed on your phone.

Your choices

Pause or delete any reminder, clear your history in the app, change or remove permissions in Android Settings, or uninstall the app to remove all its data.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information.

Contact

Questions: open an issue on the app's GitHub page or use the developer contact on Google Play.
